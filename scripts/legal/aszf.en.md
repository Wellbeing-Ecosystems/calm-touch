## 3. The service

3.1. **Calm-Touch session.**

- Gentle touch on the head, shoulders and arms, 15–20 minutes per participant, seated, through clothing, without oil, at the Client's premises.
- A session lasts at least 3 hours with up to 8 participants. For larger teams, 2 × 3 hours with up to 16 participants.
- Sessions can be weekly, every 2 or 3 weeks, or monthly.

3.2. **Wellbeing Day.** A 2 × 3-hour highlight programme for a health day, an event or a leadership day.

3.3. **Calm-Touch Pulse.**

- A short, anonymous questionnaire completed on a phone before and after each session, and once a month.
- The Client receives a monthly group-level report based on it. The Client receives no individual results, and team-level results only for groups of at least 5 people.

3.4. **Pulse+ instrumental assessment.**

- A one-off, individual assessment: brain activity (qEEG), heart rate variability (HRV) and a cognitive test, with a personal evaluation.
- Pulse+ can only be carried out with the participant's personal, written consent.
- The individual result belongs to the participant. The Client only receives combined results, and only if data from at least 10 participants are behind them.
- Fees and conditions are set out in an individual quote.

3.5. **Not a healthcare service.** Calm Touch is a service that supports wellbeing at work.

- It is not medical treatment, therapeutic massage or psychological care, and does not replace them.
- The Pulse questionnaire and the Pulse+ assessment do not provide a medical diagnosis.

9.1. **Participation is voluntary.** Participants can stop the session at any time with one word, and can say if the pressure or the area being touched is not comfortable.

9.2. **Through clothing.** Touch takes place only through clothing, on agreed areas of the head, shoulders and arms.

9.3. **Please tell the practitioner beforehand if:**

- there is a recent injury, surgery or skin problem in the area to be touched;
- you have an acute illness or a fever;
- you are pregnant;
- you do not want an area to be touched for any other reason.

The practitioner will then leave out that area or not carry out the session. We do not record this.

9.4. The practitioner may refuse or stop the session if it is not advisable because of the participant's health, or if the participant breaches the rules of mutual respect.
