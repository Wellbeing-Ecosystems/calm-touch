Effective from 24 October 2026

This statement explains what data Calm Touch uses and for what purpose. The most important point: **we do not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number to take part in Calm Touch sessions or to complete the Calm-Touch Pulse questionnaires.** We only process personal data where it cannot be avoided, and each such case is marked separately below.

## 1. In brief

- **No personal data for sessions.** There is no registration for taking part, and we do not ask for a list of names.
- **The Pulse questionnaire is anonymous.** Respondents are identified only by a four-digit code they choose themselves. Completing it is voluntary and is not a condition of taking part.
- **The company never sees individual answers.** The client company only receives combined results for groups of at least 5 people.
- **No tracking on the website.** There is no analytics, no advertising cookie and no social media plug-in. We do not ask for cookie consent because there is nothing to consent to.
- **Pulse+ instrumental assessment is the exception.** It requires personal data, so it only takes place with the participant's separate, personal consent. Here too, the company receives no individual results, only combined results for at least 10 people.
- **Beyond this, we only process personal data for business contact:**
  - quote requests and registrations;
  - the contact details of the client company's contact person (for sending reports);
  - contracts and invoicing;
  - the names of our own practitioners and project managers.

## 3. Where we do not ask for or record personal data

### 3.1 Browsing the website

- There is no registration or user account (except for the internal admin area).
- There is no analytics (e.g. Google Analytics), no advertising or tracking cookie, and no Facebook, Instagram or other plug-in.
- Fonts are loaded from our own server, so your browser does not send any data to Google when you open the website.
- The hosting provider (section 5) keeps a technical log (e.g. IP address, time, browser type, page opened) for operation and to fend off attacks. The provider keeps it for a short time under its own rules. We do not identify anyone from it and do not link it to questionnaire answers.

### 3.2 Taking part in a Calm Touch session

- We do not ask for a name, contact details or health data to take part.
- The client company schedules the time slots in its own system. We do not ask for a list of names for this. If the company does send us a schedule, we use it only to run the session and delete it afterwards.
- If a participant tells the practitioner verbally that an area should be avoided (e.g. because of a recent injury), we do not write this down.

### 3.3 Calm-Touch Pulse questionnaires (snapshot and monthly questionnaire)

Participants open the questionnaire on their own phone using the QR code displayed on site.

**What we record:**

- the four-digit code chosen by the respondent, used only to pair the before and after answers and to make sure the monthly questionnaire is completed only once a month;
- the team, if the respondent selects one (optional);
- the answers (on a 1–10 scale) and the optional single word or short comment;
- the time of completion and the time of consent;
- the session, the company and the practitioner, which are identified by the QR code, not by the respondent.

**What we do not record:** name, e-mail address, phone number, date of birth, job title, device ID or location. The questionnaire database does not store IP addresses. The database provider may keep a technical log of requests for a short time, but we do not link it to the answers.

**Please do not write names or health information in the text fields.** If any appear, we leave them out of the report or delete them.

**Why we still handle the data carefully:** No one can be identified directly from the answers. In a small team, however, the code, the team and the time of the session could in theory make an answer traceable. We therefore treat the data as pseudonymised personal data. Answers about wellbeing are protected in the same way as special categories of data.

- **Legal basis:** the respondent's explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a) GDPR), given by ticking the box at the start of the questionnaire.
- **Purpose:** measuring the effect of the programme and preparing a monthly, group-level report for the client company.
- **Who sees it:**
  - individual, anonymous answers are seen only by designated Calm Touch staff, in a password-protected area;
  - the client company receives combined results only, and team-level results only if at least 5 people answered;
  - practitioners may only receive a combined evaluation of their own sessions.
- **Retention:** 12 months from recording. The data are then deleted or irreversibly turned into combined statistics.
- **Withdrawing consent:** at any time, without giving a reason. Because we record answers without names, you will need to give your four-digit code, the company name and the date of the session. We then delete the answers linked to that code. Withdrawal does not affect reports that have already been combined.

### 3.4 Data on your own phone (local storage)

- **ct-lang:** the language you chose (Hungarian, English or German), so that the page appears in it next time.
- **ct-pre-…:** your snapshot before-answers and code, for up to 12 hours, so you can continue after the session even if the page reloads. It is deleted automatically when the after-answers are sent or after 12 hours.

These data are stored by the browser on your phone. They are not cookies, we do not send them to anyone, and you can remove them at any time by clearing your browser data.

### 3.5 Cookies

The public website and the questionnaires do not use cookies. Only the internal admin area uses two strictly necessary cookies:

- **ct_admin:** login, valid for 12 hours;
- **ct_admin_lang:** admin language, valid for 1 year.

No consent is required for these (Section 155(4) of Hungarian Act C of 2003 on electronic communications).

## 4. Where we process personal data

### 4.1 Calm-Touch Pulse+ instrumental assessment

Pulse+ is a one-off, individual assessment. It requires personal data, which is why it is the exception.

- **Data:**
  - the participant's name or identifier, so that the individual result can be assigned;
  - the measurement data: brain activity (qEEG), heart rate variability (HRV) and cognitive test results;
  - the time of the measurement.
- **Legal basis:** the participant's explicit, personal consent (Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). Before the measurement, the participant receives written information about who carries it out, what data are recorded and how long they are kept. The measurement only starts after they have signed their consent. Participation is voluntary.
- **Who sees it:**
  - the individual result is known only to the participant and to the specialist who carries out and evaluates the measurement;
  - the client company receives no individual results, only combined results, and only if data from at least 10 participants are behind them.
- **Retention:** for the period stated in the consent, but no longer than 12 months from the measurement unless the participant agrees to longer retention.
- **Withdrawal:** consent can be withdrawn at any time at {{EMAIL}}. We then delete the individual data.
- Pulse+ data are not added to the website or to the Pulse questionnaire database.

### 4.2 Quote requests, registration and the client company's contact person

If you request a quote or register on behalf of your company, or if your company names a contact person as a client, we process the following data:

- **Contact person's data:** name, job title, e-mail address, phone number and the content of messages.
- **Company data:** company name, industry, total number of employees and region (county). These are not personal data.
- **Where we get them from:**
  - the quote request or registration form operated by bevetel.hu (the bevetel.hu privacy notice also applies to the data entered there);
  - e-mail or phone;
  - the Client when the contract is concluded.
- **If the company names another employee as contact person,** the company must inform them. We also inform them when we first contact them, and they can ask for their data to be corrected or deleted at any time.
- **What we use them for:**
  - preparing the quote;
  - organising sessions;
  - sending the monthly Pulse report;
  - communication about the contract.

  We do not send newsletters or advertising, and we do not pass the data on to third parties.
- **Where we store them:** in the password-protected Calm Touch admin area, in a database located in the European Union (Ireland). Only Calm Touch administrators have access.
- **Legal basis:** steps prior to entering into a contract and performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and our legitimate interest in keeping in contact with companies (Article 6(1)(f)).
- **Retention:**
  - if no contract is concluded, 1 year from the last contact;
  - if a contract is concluded, for its duration and for 5 years afterwards, which is the limitation period for civil-law claims.

  If the company names a new contact person, we delete the previous contact person's data.
- **Anonymous combined statistics:** we use the company's industry, size category and region, together with Pulse results, only when combined with data from other companies in anonymous statistics. These only show combined results of at least 3 companies, so no single company can be identified. Contact person data are never included.

### 4.3 Contract and invoicing

- **Data:** the client company's details, the contact person's name and contact details, and invoice data.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and, for invoices, a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).
- **Retention:** accounting documents are kept for 8 years under Section 169 of Hungarian Act C of 2000 on accounting.

### 4.4 Our practitioners and project managers

- **Data:**
  - the names of practitioners, so that sessions and their combined evaluations can be assigned to them (internal quality assurance);
  - the names of the project manager(s) who lead a company's project on the delivery side.
- **Legal basis:** performance of our cooperation agreement with them and our legitimate interest in the quality of the service.
- **Who sees it:** Calm Touch administrators only. The client company may also learn the project manager's name.
- **Retention:** for the duration of the cooperation and for up to 12 months afterwards.
